# Acme Corp Grade Entitlements Matrix

Fictional company. Synthetic document written for a competition prototype; it is not any real organisation's policy.

## 1. Grade bands

1.1 Grades E1, E2 and E3 are Executive grades. Grades M1, M2 and M3 are Manager grades. Grades D1, D2 and D3 are Director grades.

## 2. Eligible expense categories

2.1 Executive grades are eligible to claim meals, local transport, fuel, communication, office supplies and training.

2.2 Manager grades are eligible to claim everything available to Executive grades, and additionally lodging and client entertainment.

2.3 Director grades are eligible to claim every category available to Manager grades.

## 3. Communication allowance

3.1 Mobile and broadband bills are reimbursable up to INR 800 per month for Executive grades.

3.2 Mobile and broadband bills are reimbursable up to INR 1,200 per month for Manager and Director grades.
