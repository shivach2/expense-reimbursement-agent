# Acme Corp Travel and Expense Policy

Fictional company. Synthetic document written for a competition prototype; it is not any real organisation's policy.
Version 3.1, effective 1 January 2026.

## 1. General conditions

1.1 Expense receipts must be submitted within 60 days of the date of the expense. Receipts older than 60 days are not reimbursable.

1.2 Every reimbursement claim must state the business purpose of the expense.

1.3 Personal expenses, medical expenses, fines and penalties are not reimbursable under any circumstances.

1.4 All limits in this policy are in Indian Rupees (INR). Bills in other currencies are converted to INR before the limits are applied.

## 2. Lodging

2.1 Hotel accommodation is reimbursable up to INR 5,000 per night.

2.2 If a hotel bill exceeds the nightly limit, the employee bears the excess and the company reimburses the amount up to the limit. Partial reimbursement is permitted for lodging.

## 3. Meals

3.1 Meals during business travel are reimbursable up to INR 1,500 per receipt.

3.2 Meals above this limit are not reimbursed unless the employee's manager approves an exception, in which case the justification must be recorded with the claim.

## 4. Local transport and fuel

4.1 Local taxi, cab, parking and public transport are reimbursable up to INR 3,000 per day.

4.2 Fuel for a personal vehicle used on official duty is reimbursable up to INR 2,500 per receipt. A fuel receipt is mandatory.

## 5. Client entertainment

5.1 Meals and entertainment for clients are reimbursable up to INR 3,000 per receipt. The claim must name the clients hosted.

## 6. Training and conferences

6.1 Training courses and conference fees are reimbursable up to INR 15,000 per receipt, and require prior written approval from the employee's manager.

## 7. Office supplies

7.1 Office supplies are reimbursable up to INR 1,000 per receipt.
